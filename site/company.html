Our story

Mighty products. Serious service.

MightyFix is a sealants and adhesives brand made for real work. It started with one simple idea: products should do the job right the first time.

Today the range covers sealants, adhesives, expanding foams, tapes and floor preparation, for construction, manufacturing and the home.

Whether it's a contractor sealing a bathroom on a tight deadline or a homeowner fixing a leaking window on a Saturday, our products are made to get it done.
